Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Pearl Drift Pvt Ltd (“Pearl Drift”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a travel business based in the Maldives. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit www.pearldrifttours.com, contact us, enquire about a trip, or make a booking. We aim to collect only information that is reasonably needed for these purposes and to handle it responsibly.

This policy describes our intended practices. The exact information processed will depend on how you interact with us and which services you choose to use.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Pearl Drift Pvt Ltd is responsible for personal information that we collect directly for our own business purposes. Some services involved in a booking—such as accommodation, transport, payment services, messaging platforms or other travel suppliers—may process information under their own privacy terms.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on your enquiry or booking, we may receive or collect:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, telephone number and preferred contact method.
  • Enquiry and booking details: travel dates, destinations, party size, requested activities, itinerary choices, preferences and correspondence with us.
  • Traveller details: passport or identity information, nationality, date of birth or other travel-document details only where reasonably needed for a booking, supplier, or legal requirement.
  • Payment and transaction information: payment status, amount, transaction references and information needed to reconcile a payment. If a third-party payment provider is used, that provider may collect payment-card or bank details directly under its own privacy policy.
  • Technical information: basic information that may be supplied by your browser or hosting infrastructure, such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and access times, for security, operation or troubleshooting.
  • Website preference: this website may use browser local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference.

Please do not send passport details, payment credentials or other sensitive information through an ordinary message unless we have explained why it is needed and provided an appropriate way to send it.

3. How we use information

We may use personal information to:

  • respond to questions and prepare quotes or proposed itineraries;
  • manage bookings, confirmations, payments, changes, cancellations and customer support;
  • pass relevant traveller details to accommodation, transport operators, guides and other suppliers when needed to deliver the service;
  • meet applicable legal, accounting, immigration, safety or regulatory requirements;
  • protect our business, customers and website, and investigate suspected fraud or misuse;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and improve our services; and
  • send marketing messages where permitted and, where required, with your consent.

We do not intentionally collect personal information simply because you browse the website, beyond information that may be processed by the website host or your browser as part of delivering and securing the site.

4. Our reasons for processing information

As applicable to the circumstances, we process information because it is needed to respond to your request or perform a booking, because we must comply with a legal obligation, because you have given consent, or because we have a legitimate business need such as protecting the website, preventing fraud or maintaining records. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it for future processing; this does not make earlier processing unlawful and may not affect processing that has another valid basis.

Privacy and data-protection requirements can depend on the relevant country and circumstances. The Maldives was considering comprehensive personal-data protection legislation in 2026; we will review and update our practices if applicable law changes. If data-protection laws in another jurisdiction apply to a particular interaction, we will handle the information in accordance with the obligations that apply to us.

5. When we share information

We may share only the information reasonably needed with:

  • travel suppliers and service providers involved in delivering your requested booking;
  • payment providers, banks or financial institutions where relevant to a transaction;
  • website hosting, IT, communications or other service providers that help us operate our business;
  • government, immigration, tourism, law-enforcement or other authorities where required or permitted by law; and
  • professional advisers or other parties where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims or protect people, rights or property.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not authorise service providers who process information on our behalf to use it for unrelated purposes, subject to their own legal obligations and the terms governing their services.

6. External websites and messaging services

Our website or communications may direct you to external websites or services, including email, WhatsApp, Telegram, social networks, booking suppliers or payment providers. When you use those services, their operators may process your information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control their independent data practices. Please review their policies before submitting information to them.

7. International transfers

Travel arrangements and online service providers may involve processing or storing information outside the Maldives or your country of residence. Where we arrange or make such a transfer, we will take reasonable steps to use appropriate providers and safeguards and to comply with any applicable legal requirements. The laws protecting information in another country may differ from those in your home country.

8. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including managing your booking, responding to a dispute, maintaining business and accounting records, and meeting legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the circumstances. When information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or remove identifying details, unless we are required or permitted to retain it for longer.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

This website may use browser local storage to remember your display-theme preference. We do not intentionally use analytics or advertising cookies unless such tools are added to the website and this policy is updated accordingly. Your browser may also process technical data, and the website host may maintain security or access logs. You can manage local storage and cookies through your browser settings; clearing them may remove saved preferences or affect how the site behaves.

10. Keeping information secure

We take reasonable steps appropriate to a small travel business to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. These steps may include limiting access, using reputable service providers and avoiding unnecessary collection of sensitive details. No website, messaging channel, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please take care when sending information online.

11. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, ask us to delete information that we no longer need, object to or ask us to limit certain uses, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. The availability and scope of these rights depend on applicable law, and we may need to retain some information to complete a booking, meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute.

You can opt out of marketing messages at any time by following the opt-out instructions provided or contacting us. We may still send non-marketing communications needed to manage an existing enquiry or booking.

To protect your information, we may ask for reasonable details to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable law.

12. Children’s information

Our services are intended to be arranged by adults or by a parent or legal guardian on behalf of a child where appropriate. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information for independent marketing. If traveller information about a child is needed for a booking, it should be provided by the child’s parent or legal guardian or another person authorised to make the booking. Please contact us if you believe a child’s information has been provided to us inappropriately.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology or applicable law. We will update the date at the top when we make a revision. The updated version will apply from the time it is published on this website. Please review this page periodically.
